Music from your town, and everywhere else.

Milk Crate is a free music app built as a nonprofit. Find the bands in your scene, keep your records and CDs next to your streams, and when you pay, your money goes to the artists you actually play.

Download for Windows Download for Mac

Free for Windows 10 and 11, and macOS. No ads, ever.

Your money should reach the bands you play.

Most streaming services put every subscription into one big pot and split it by total plays across the whole service. If you spend the month on three bands from your town, most of your money still ends up with the most-streamed artists in the world.

Milk Crate splits what you pay between the artists you listened to, keeps only what it costs to run, and publishes the numbers every year. You can also buy an album outright, or pay an artist once to become their Fan and get everything they release.

What we promise.

These are in Milk Crate's terms, not just on this page. If one ever changes, you'll hear about it 30 days ahead and can leave instead.

  • No ads.Not in the app, not between songs, not ever.
  • Your information isn't for sale.We never sell or rent it, or share it for advertising.
  • Nobody pays to be promoted.No paid placement for artists, labels or songs. Discovery favors local and up-and-coming artists, and there are no top-100 charts.
  • Artists come first in the split.When you pay, your money goes to the artists you listen to, and we publish how it was divided every year.
  • Full-quality downloads.Download whole albums to play offline in the app, at full quality. Artists can't switch that off.
  • If it closes, nobody cashes out.Artists get at least 30 days to take their work, and anything left goes to another nonprofit, never to a person.

Like digging through a record store's crates.

Your scene

Set your city and Milk Crate builds your scene: towns within 35 miles plus your state's bigger cities, with every band on a real map.

Your crate

Save albums, make playlists, and catalog the vinyl, cassettes and CDs on your shelf. Rip your own CDs to lossless FLAC; they stay on your computer.

Stars that steer shuffle

Rate albums and songs from one to five. Shuffle leans toward what you love, and nothing gets ranked on a chart.

Pages artists design

Every artist designs their own page: banner, lettering, city and genre. You'll find who they sound like from how their songs actually sound.

Sounds the way you want

A six-band equalizer with presets, and an immersive mode that fills the screen with the album and its colors.

Keeps itself current

Milk Crate tells you when a new version is ready and updates in one click.

Making music?

Download Milk Crate and apply for an artist account. A person reviews every new artist before their music goes public, so the bins stay worth digging through.

  • You keep every right to your music and artwork.
  • Take a release down whenever you want.
  • Sell or share your music anywhere else you like.
  • Upload WAV, AIFF or FLAC; listeners stream high-quality copies.
  • When listeners pay, it goes to the artists they play, not a shared pool.

Get Milk Crate.

It's free. You'll need an account to save music and follow artists: you must be 13 or older, and under 18 with a parent's permission.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11

  1. Download the installer and open it.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the installer, then open Milk Crate from the Start menu.
Download for Windows

Mac

Apple silicon and Intel

  1. Download the disk image, open it, and drag Milk Crate into Applications.
  2. Open Milk Crate. If macOS won't open it, go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.
  3. You only do this once.
